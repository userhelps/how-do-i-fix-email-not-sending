How Do I Fix Email Not Sending? - Email Troubleshooting Guide

Having trouble sending emails? Messages can sometimes remain in the outbox, fail to send, or display an error when you try to send them. This guide explains common causes of email sending problems and provides practical steps to help get your email working again.

Common Email Sending Problems

  • Email messages remain stuck in the Outbox.
  • Emails fail to send or return an error message.
  • Attachments prevent messages from being sent.
  • Your email app is not connecting to the mail server.
  • Your internet connection is unavailable or unstable.
  • Your email account requires you to sign in again.
  • Outgoing mail server settings may be incorrect.

How Do I Fix Email Not Sending?

Quick Troubleshooting Steps:

  1. Check that your device has a working internet connection.
  2. Open your email app or website and confirm that you are signed in.
  3. Check the Outbox for messages that are stuck waiting to send.
  4. Try sending a short test email without an attachment.
  5. Verify that the recipient's email address is entered correctly.
  6. Close and reopen your email app.
  7. Restart your phone, tablet, or computer.
  8. Try sending the email again.

Check Your Internet Connection

A weak or disconnected internet connection can prevent an email from being sent. Open a web browser and visit another website to confirm that your internet connection is working.

  • Check that WiFi or mobile data is enabled.
  • Restart your router if your internet connection is unavailable.
  • Try switching between WiFi and mobile data when possible.
  • Reconnect to your wireless network if the connection has dropped.

Check the Email Address

Before Sending:

  1. Check the recipient's email address for spelling mistakes.
  2. Make sure the address contains the correct domain name.
  3. Remove any accidental spaces from the email address.
  4. Try sending the message to another trusted address as a test.

Check Email Attachments

Large attachments or unsupported file types can sometimes prevent an email from being sent. If your message contains an attachment, remove it and try sending the email again.

Tip: If the email sends successfully without the attachment, the file size or file type may be causing the problem. Consider compressing the file or using a supported cloud-storage sharing option instead.

Check Your Outbox

If an email is stuck in your Outbox, it may prevent the app from completing the sending process.

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Open the Outbox folder.
  3. Check for messages marked as pending or unsent.
  4. Open the stuck message and review it for errors.
  5. Try sending it again or remove it and create a new message.

Check Your Email Account Settings

If basic troubleshooting does not work, your account may need to be authenticated again or your outgoing mail settings may need attention.

  • Confirm that your email username is correct.
  • Sign in again if your email provider requests authentication.
  • Check that your outgoing mail server settings are correct.
  • Make sure your account has not been temporarily restricted.
  • Check your email provider's service status if available.

What If Email Still Will Not Send?

Important: Avoid repeatedly changing account settings unless you know the correct configuration. Incorrect mail-server settings can prevent your email application from connecting to your provider.
  • Restart your device and try again.
  • Try accessing your email through a web browser.
  • Check whether other emails can be sent from the same account.
  • Remove a problematic attachment and resend the message.
  • Check your email provider for service interruptions.
  • Contact your email provider if the account remains unable to send messages.

Email Sends From Browser but Not From App

If you can successfully send email through your provider's website but cannot send messages through your email application, the issue may be related to the app's account configuration, authentication, or outgoing mail settings.

Try These Steps:

  1. Close and reopen the email application.
  2. Check that the correct email account is selected.
  3. Verify the account's outgoing mail settings.
  4. Update the email application if an update is available.
  5. Remove and re-add the email account only if necessary and if you know the correct login details.

Final Advice

Get Your Email Sending Again

When email is not sending, start by checking your internet connection, recipient address, Outbox, attachments, and account sign-in status. Restarting the email app or device can also resolve temporary problems. If the issue continues, check your provider's service status or contact your email provider for account-specific assistance.
